# 🏥 CareHub – Healthcare Appointment Management System

CareHub is a web-based healthcare appointment management system that helps patients find available doctors, view their schedules, describe their health concerns, and book appointments. An admin manages doctor availability, appointment details, and alternative doctor assignments when a preferred doctor is unavailable.

## ✨ Features

- 👨‍⚕️ **Doctor Directory** – View available doctors and their details.
- 📅 **Doctor Availability** – Check doctors' available dates and schedules.
- 🩺 **Health Concern Submission** – Describe the reason for requesting an appointment.
- 📋 **Appointment Booking** – Request appointments based on doctor availability.
- 🔄 **Alternative Doctor Selection** – Find another available doctor when the preferred doctor is unavailable.
- 🛠️ **Admin Dashboard** – Manage doctors, schedules, and patient appointments.
- 📊 **Appointment Management** – Track and update appointment statuses.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Patients visit CareHub and browse the doctor directory.
2. They check doctor availability and schedules.
3. Patients enter their health concerns and select a suitable appointment date.
4. The system checks the selected doctor's availability.
5. If the doctor is unavailable, patients can choose another available doctor.
6. The admin manages doctor schedules, reviews appointment requests, and updates appointment statuses.
7. Patients can view appointment details and status, if supported by the application.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The goal of CareHub is to simplify healthcare appointment management by connecting patients with available doctors, improving schedule visibility, reducing appointment conflicts, and helping administrators coordinate appointments efficiently.
